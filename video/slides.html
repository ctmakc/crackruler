Limits and responsible use

  • Metric accuracy is measured on synthetic scenes; field photos with comparator-card readings come next.
  • ACI 224R-01 Table 4.1 routes a finding. It never certifies a structure as safe.
  • Photos are processed in memory and never stored; reports expire after 7 days.
  • Every wide, uncertain or unresolvable case ends with a person.
